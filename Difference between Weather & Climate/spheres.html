The Difference between Weather & Climate Summary & References

Weather and climate are really closely similar, but they ain't the same thing. Weather is something you can do it your-self like you can just look out the window and check if it is sunny, cloudy, rainy, or windy; weather is like what we feel and see in real life in like hours or minutes. On the other hand, climate can be said to be a prediction for a specific place based on a long period of time.

Climate scientists, who look at averages of wind speed, rainfall, sunshine, temperature, and other smaller factors that they found along the 30 years of research; even though weather can change really fast, the long term climate helps scientists tell them the trend and patterns of the weather of decades or even more. Climate is to provide an idea of the climate in the seasons or years, but the climate can't say the weather of no specific date; Climate is for telling you an idea of temperature in a month like September in Wellington, but not a defined number just a margin.
