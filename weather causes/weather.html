Extreme Weather

Causes of Weather Summary & References

The cause of the weather is by uneven heating in Earth’s surface by the sun, this results in different temperatures in different locations. Air pressure affects the weather too because, if a place has a higher/lower pressure it will make wind that brings the temperature down. Plus, vertical motion creates rising air and cools down that leads to condensation, precipitation and cloud making.
